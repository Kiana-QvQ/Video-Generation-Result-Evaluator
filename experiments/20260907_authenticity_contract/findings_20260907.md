# Frozen V3 Diagnostic Findings

Date: 2026-09-07

## Scope

All runs used the frozen `wangxing_v3_res1k.pt` checkpoint. No production
model, inference implementation, profile, flag, or final test asset was
changed by these experiments.

## Development Scan

`scan_v3_development_holdout.py` evaluated the existing V3 official
development holdout (50 real, 50 generated):

| Metric | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Generated recall | 98.0% (49/50) |
| Real recall | 96.0% (48/50) |
| Accuracy | 97.0% (97/100) |
| False positives | 2 |
| False negatives | 1 |

The three errors are confidently wrong rather than threshold-adjacent:

| Case | Ground truth | `p_gen` | AU quality |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `CL_beishang01/049090310172` | real | 0.9764 | 1.0 |
| `CL_kaixin01/049032710126` | real | 0.7541 | 0.0 |
| `video_UIFtwobcEB7RosAMxNsLHrFUqurplU9L` | generated | 0.0326 | 1.0 |

Therefore a single threshold move cannot repair the errors: it cannot correct
the AI false negative without admitting the two real false positives, and it
cannot correct the real false positives without losing the AI false negative.

## Locality Probe

The face/background probe exposes distinct failure mechanisms:

| Case | Original | Face blur | Background blur | Reading |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `beishang01` real FP | 0.9764 | 0.0319 | 0.9751 | Face-local texture or motion evidence is the trigger. |
| `kaixin01` real FP | 0.7541 | 0.7541 | 0.7541 | Not explained by this face/background split; inspect codec, temporal pattern, and source coverage. |
| Seedance AI FN | 0.0326 | 0.9333 | 0.0350 | The model's real-like evidence is face-local; removing the face restores AI evidence. |

The neutral `face_only` and `background_only` outputs are intentionally used
only as diagnostic perturbations. They are not valid online inputs.

## V3 Branch Probe

| Case | Joint | Video auxiliary | AU auxiliary | Joint with AU zeroed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `beishang01` real FP | 0.9764 | 0.9310 | 0.4865 | 0.9772 |
| `kaixin01` real FP | 0.7541 | 0.6671 | 0.3675 | 0.9288 |
| Seedance AI FN | 0.0326 | 0.1008 | 0.5409 | 0.0321 |

Conclusions:

1. The errors are already primarily video-branch errors; identity, expression,
   and display/ranking evidence are not an appropriate correction signal.
2. `au_quality_min == 0` is a useful monitoring slice but does not justify an
   "AU missing -> video only" rule. On the `kaixin01` real false positive,
   zeroing AU increases `p_gen` from 0.7541 to 0.9288.
3. Auxiliary heads are useful diagnostic evidence but are not calibrated,
   independently validated replacement classifiers.

## Candidate To Train After Data Expansion

Do not add a handwritten override. The next candidate should be a separate
forensic fusion model with:

- Inputs allowed: frozen V3 logit, independent face-temporal forensic score,
  global temporal/codec score, AU coverage/quality, and perturbation
  stability features.
- Inputs prohibited: identity similarity, expression compatibility, ranking,
  or any display-derived score.
- Training protocol: grouped source/generator split; preserve full V3
  training data isolation; build a dedicated V3 failure-development set; keep
  25x25 and 32x32 final sets untouched until one final pass.
- Decision contract: a candidate may automatically add generated evidence
  only after independent validation. It must never turn a V3-generated result
  into real merely because identity, expression, or ranking looks favorable.

At the current sample size, no trained replacement is defensible. The
immediate data collection target is multiple real clips matching the
face-local/low-texture false-positive conditions, plus multiple generated
clips with real-like face-local behavior from generators beyond Seedance.
